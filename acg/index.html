How a project runs

  1. Walk through the task

    A 30-minute call where you show how the work gets done today: the files, the people and how often it happens.

  2. Get a written proposal

    Scope, price and timeline in writing before any work starts.

  3. Build and test with your data

    Check-ins as it comes together, tested against real examples from your team.

  4. Hand-off and training

    You own the files. Your team gets a written guide and a walkthrough.
