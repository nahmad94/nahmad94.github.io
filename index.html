Franchise P&L tracker

Regional seafood and chicken franchisor, 140+ locations

The problem
Ownership had no consistent, location-level view of sales, labor and food costs across corporate and franchisee stores.
What I built
An Excel/VBA workbook shared through Google Cloud. Each manager enters sales, payroll, food and operating costs; the workbook calculates P&L, food cost % and year-over-year snapshots.
Result
Adopted by every corporate location and most franchisee locations. It cut food waste and exposed cash shortages and theft.

Session analytics dashboard

Association annual symposium

The problem
The client's event data sat in four separate platform exports that were hard to read side by side.
What I built
An interactive HTML dashboard combining session tracking, survey summaries, presentation ratings and speaker assignments.
Result
Delivered to the client as a single view of how each session performed.

iMIS member lookup and write-back

Association registration sites

The problem
Registrants had to be matched to their association membership record before they could see the right options.
What I built
A lookup against the client's iMIS (ASI) IQA REST endpoint that returns company and member type by email, plus a write-back integration coordinated with the client's third-party API manager.

Frac fleet field tracker

Pump-down services company, Permian Basin

The problem
Stage data and billing were recorded by hand at the wellsite, then retyped into invoices.
What I built
An Excel/VBA tracker capturing stage data, fluid volumes, proppant, pressures and pump rates, with automated per-product pricing that produced customer-signed field tickets on location.
Result
Used across 3 frac fleets.
